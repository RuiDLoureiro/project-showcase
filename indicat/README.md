# Indicat

**Stage:** Android competition prototype, 2019  
**Technology:** Android Studio · Firebase

Indicat explored how a customer could find a restaurant and open its menu on a phone. The recorded prototype demonstrates restaurant browsing, selection and menu display.

## Contribution

I developed the product concept, Android prototype, Firebase integration and competition demonstration. The customer flow separated restaurant discovery from the menu viewer so the same viewer could serve different entry points.

## Design decisions

The prototype displayed existing menu images, preserving the restaurant's layout and reducing the work needed to recreate menu content. This approach also limits text search and can require zooming on a phone. QR access and an owner workflow for uploading menu files were part of the broader design; the recorded demo establishes the browsing flow.

## Result

The project produced an Android prototype and recorded demonstration. [CDI Portugal lists Indicat among the projects awarded €1,500 for further development at Transforma TI Valongo 2019](https://cdi.org.pt/en/evento-final-da-2a-edicao-do-centro-de-cidadania-digital-de-valongo-2-2/).
