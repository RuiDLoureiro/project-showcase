# poise.

**Stage:** Prototype design; physical validation pending

poise. is a pendant concept for posture awareness during everyday activities. The intended interaction is a discreet tactile reminder after the wearer remains outside a personally chosen reference.

## Design work

I explored product requirements, compact electronics packaging, low-power operation, tactile feedback and a charging dock. The design balances perceptible feedback against unwanted alerts, and a small enclosure against practical assembly and comfort.

## What still needs testing

An assembled prototype is needed to measure repeatability, false alerts, energy use, charging and comfort. Product visualisations show the intended exterior and use. Battery life, manufacturing cost and health outcomes have not been established.
